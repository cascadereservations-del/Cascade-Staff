Welcome

What Cascade Hideaway is, who does what, and how to use this guide.

Cascade Hideaway is a small boutique stay in General Santos City. Our promise to every guest is Hotel Comfort. Home Warmth. This guide shows you how the team keeps that promise, one task at a time.

What Cascade Hideaway is

Cascade is one bedroom with a queen bed and a single pull-out bed, one bathroom and a full kitchen. It sleeps up to three guests. Guests book on our booking site, on Messenger or on Airbnb.

Behind the stay there is a team and a few simple apps. You do not need to know how the apps work inside. You only need to know which one to open, what to tap and what to do when something looks wrong.

Every task in this guide comes back to the promise. Clean like a hotel. Welcome like a home.

If you join the team, Lloyd will give you your own sign-ins.

How to use this guide

  1. Search. Press Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac) to search the whole guide for a word, a button or a step.
  2. Pick your role. At the top, choose Cleaner or Office. The guide then shows the chapters for that work. Chapters 0 to 2, 8, 9 and 11 to 13 are for everyone.
  3. Follow a walkthrough. A walkthrough shows one step at a time with a progress bar. Tap Done when you finish. The guide remembers it on this device.
  4. Tap the pins on a screenshot. Each numbered pin tells you what a button does, who can use it, and whether you can undo it. The same list is printed under the picture.
  5. Try the Telegram cards. The cards in this guide are copies. You can tap their buttons to see what would happen. Nothing is sent.
  6. Look up a word. A marked word such as turnover shows its meaning when you tap or hover on it. All words are also listed at the end of Policies and glossary.

Your ticks and walkthrough progress are saved in this browser only. They do not follow you to another phone.

PIN and password rules

  1. Your PIN or password is yours only. Every person has their own.
  2. Never share it. Never lend your phone while you are signed in.
  3. Never write it in a chat, a message, a photo or a note. No one on the team will ask you for it.
  4. If you forget it, or think someone saw it, tell Lloyd right away. He will reset it.

This guide never shows a PIN, a door code or a Wi-Fi password. If someone asks you to type one into a chat, do not do it. Tell Lloyd.

Doing a turnover with the Cleaner app

Phase 0 to 4 in the Cleaner app, step by step, with photos, meters, supply log and submit.

You do every cleaning in the Cleaner app. It walks you through five screens, one after the other. The app will not let you skip a required step, so you cannot forget the important things.

Open the app here: Cleaner app. Sign in with your own name and PIN. Your PIN is personal: never write it down and never share it.

The top of the app says "Phase 1 of 5" on the first screen. This guide starts at Phase 0 so it matches the Manual and the office. Phase 0 here is the app's first screen, Setup.

The whole turnover in one look

Tap through the steps below to get the big picture. Each panel is one screen of the app. The details for each screen come after.

Sign in

Sign in with your own name and PIN. The app says "Signed in as" with your name. If it is not you, tap Switch account.

About 30 seconds. Choose the Cleaning Type. Pick the stay in Which stay is this report for?. Check the date. Tap Next →.

Phase 1 - Document

About 3 minutes, before you touch anything. Take the pre-cleaning photos. Type the two meter readings and photograph both meters. Say if the unit is All Clear or Found Something.

Open up, check the unit, and spray the chemicals. Start the timer with ▶ Start Timer. Do other jobs while the chemicals soak. Do not scrub yet.

Clean the bedroom first, then the kitchen and bathroom. Take at least 2 kitchen photos. Then open the supply log.

Do the final cleaning. Answer every Confirm & Leave item. Follow the power and safety instructions. Take the after-cleaning photos.

Submit

Tap Submit ✓. A box called Submit report? opens. Read it. Tap Submit ✓ again. Keep the app open until you see Report Submitted.

Cleaning types

In Phase 0 you choose the type of cleaning. The type changes what the app asks for.

TypeWhen you use itWhat changes
Turnover (the default) A guest has checked out and a new guest arrives. Everything is required: guest name, both meters and meter photos, 5 pre-cleaning photos, 2 kitchen photos, supply log, 5 after-cleaning photos, and all six Confirm & Leave items.

Phase 0 - Setup (about 30 seconds)

Fill in the small form at the top of the app.

  2. Check "Signed in as" shows your name.
  3. Choose the Cleaning Type: Turnover, Mid-Stay, Deep Clean or Emergency.

The app has small signs to help you. A red Critical badge means safety: do not skip it. A blue Disinfect badge means use a disinfectant. A purple Biweekly badge means do it every two weeks only. On a task, the triangle button on the right lets you report damage with a note and a photo.

Phase 1 - Document (about 3 minutes)

Do this before you clean. It protects you and Cascade if there is a claim later.

Step 1: Pre-cleaning photos

  • You need at least 5 photos on a Turnover or Deep Clean. On a Mid-Stay you need 4.
  • Aim for 10 to 12. Take several angles of each area.
  • Cover these areas: Bedroom, Living Area, Kitchen, Appliances, Bathroom, Entrance.
  • Use Capture Photo to open the camera, or Upload Photo to pick from your gallery.
  • The counter shows how many you have, for example "0 / 12 photos attached".

Step 2: Meter readings

Type the electric reading (kWh) and the water reading (m³) exactly as the meters show. The app also shows the previous reading and the difference. Both readings are required on a Turnover and a Deep Clean. On a Mid-Stay they are optional.

Step 3: Meter photos

Tap Add Photo under the electric meter and again under the water meter. The photos upload by themselves in the background. You do not need internet to keep going.

Water meters are easy to misread. If the app shows Worth a second look, look at the meter again. If your reading is right as typed, carry on. This message never blocks your report. Tick Meter readings match the photos when you are sure.

If the app finds a problem with a meter photo, it may offer I could not photograph the meters. This is a meter-skip.

  • Write a few words about what happened, for example "meter room was locked".
  • Tap Submit without meter photos, just this once.
  • You can do this once. If your last report also had no meter photos, the app will not offer it. Never two in a row.
  • You still type both readings.

Step 4: Unit condition

Look around before you start. Then choose one:

  • All Clear: no damage or unusual issues.
  • Found Something: a box opens. Write what you found in Describe what you found and add a photo with Add photo (optional).

The app lets you carry on without the photo, but a report with a note and a photo is the one the office can act on. Always add both when something is wrong.

Then tap Next →. On a Turnover or Deep Clean, the app needs both meter readings, both meter photos and your pre-cleaning photos before it lets you go on.

Phase 2 - Arrive & Prep

The screen says "Open up, check the unit, spray chemicals and start the timer". Tick each task as you finish it.

  1. Under Arrive: gather your tools, open windows, turn on the lights, test the appliances, look for damage and leaks, and check the key card is on its keychain.
  2. Strip the bed and towels. Look for stains and pre-treat them.
  3. Pre-spray the toilet, shower, stove and microwave. Do not scrub yet.
  4. Pick a time: 5 min, 10 min, 15 min or 20 min. The app suggests 10 minutes.
  5. Tap ▶ Start Timer.

The dwell time is the time the chemicals need to soak. An alarm plays when it ends. Tap Alarm On to mute it.

Keep the app open while the timer runs. If you switch to another app, the timer can stop. If you never start the timer, the submit box will warn that the chemicals may not have had enough soak time.

Phase 3 - Clean

The screen says "Bedroom first, then kitchen and bathroom while chemicals soak".

  • Bedroom & Living Room: tick the tasks. Photos here are optional.
  • Kitchen & Bathroom: tick the tasks. On a Turnover or Deep Clean, photos here are required.

Kitchen photos

Take at least 2 photos. They must show the utensil drawer, the dish rack and the cookware. The office uses them to check the kitchen set against the list.

Supply log

The supply log tells the office what was used, so they can restock.

  1. Tap Open Supply Log.
  2. Use the plus and minus buttons for each item you used or refilled. Liquids are in ml.
  3. Tap Save Usage. If you used nothing, tap Nothing used this session instead.

You cannot go to the Finish phase until you save the supply log or tap Nothing used this session. The app says "save the supply log before moving to Finish".

Additional expenses (optional)

Did you spend money for the unit, for example a replacement item or a tricycle to buy supplies? Tap ＋ Add an expense. Type the amount and a short description. Both are needed. An amount with no description is not sent. Leave it empty if you spent nothing.

Phase 4 - Finish

  1. Do the Final Cleaning tasks: vacuum and mop toward the door, wipe the balcony, walk through as the guest, and put the vacuum on charge.
  2. Answer every Confirm & Leave item (see below).
  3. Follow the Power & Safety instructions (see Power and safety scenarios).
  4. Choose if you will do the weekly deep tasks (see below).
  5. Take the after-cleaning photos.
  6. Tap Submit ✓.

The heading on the screen says Confirm & Leave - All 4 required. Four is the minimum. A Turnover or Deep Clean shows more items (six). Confirm every item you see.

These are the Confirm & Leave checks. A few notes:

  • EcoFlow: it must be charged to 100% at every turnover. If it reads less, leave it on charge. Do not close the session until it is full. It stays at Cascade always.
  • Key card: tap the card on the reader. It must beep and unlock. Leave the card on the keychain inside the unit. Put the key card and both remotes back on the wooden tray on the TV cabinet. The gate pod lock stays indoors with them.
  • Guest code: enter the code from the current booking on the keypad. The lock must open cleanly. Never write the code anywhere.
  • SmartLife: open the SmartLife app, tap Smart at the bottom, then Automations at the top. Switch on "Waiting for Check In". Do this as the very last thing, after you lock up. If the switch will not stay on, tap Issue Found.

Part 1: What the guest does on the booking site

At the top of the page the guest taps Check-in and Check-out and chooses dates on the small calendar. Then they tap Check dates. The page shows if the dates are free. The calendar is the live calendar, so taken nights cannot be picked.

3. Step 2: Review & Pay

The guest sees the price, then chooses how much to pay now:

  • Reserve (50%) holds the dates now. It is a reservation fee of half the total. It is offered only when check-in is 5 days away or more.
  • Pay in Full pays everything. When check-in is less than 5 days away, this is the only choice. There is no 50% reservation.

They then pick Bank Transfer or GCash. The page shows the payment details and buttons to copy the account number and the amount.

4. Attach the receipt

When something goes wrong

Steps to follow by symptom and by alert card, and who to ask.

Most problems have a simple next step. Pick what you see. The guide tells you what to do. If it is about servers, keys or an app that will not open, the answer is always the same: tell Lloyd.

Take a screenshot before you ask for help. Write the time and what you were doing. This saves a lot of questions.

How an alert reaches you

The system checks things all day. When something is wrong, it posts a card in Telegram. The first line shows the colour and the subject. The last line starts with Do: and tells you the one thing to do.

By symptom: what is wrong?

What is wrong?

The Cleaner app will not submit.

  1. Read the warnings on the Submit screen. They say what is missing, such as an issue note, a photo that did not upload, or the timer.
  2. Check the list: at least 5 photos before cleaning, at least 2 kitchen photos, the supply log saved, every Confirm and Leave item shown (four or more, depending on the cleaning type), and at least 5 photos after cleaning.
  3. Check your signal. The app does not try again by itself. When the signal is back, tap Submit Now once and wait.
  4. Do not clear your browser data. Your draft is saved. If the app asks, tap Resume.
  5. Still stuck after two tries? Take a screenshot and tell Lloyd. The app also sends him an error card by itself.

A photo will not upload.

  1. Check your signal. Move to a place with better signal, or use Wi-Fi.
  2. Keep the app open until the photo finishes. Do not switch to another app.
  3. Take the photo again. A photo that failed shows a warning on the Submit screen.
  4. If the camera does not open, check in your phone settings that your browser may use the camera. Then reload the app.
  5. Cannot get a meter photo at all? The app allows a meter skip only now and then. If it does not allow it, take the photo. Tell Marifel what happened.

The power is out.

  1. Look in the OPS group for a brownout card or notice. If there is one, the cut is planned and nothing is broken.
  2. Planned cut: keep the emergency light close. The EcoFlow should have been charged the day before.
  3. No notice? Post in the OPS group what you see and the time. Tell Lloyd. The system does not warn about cuts that were not planned.
  4. A guest is in the unit? Tell Marifel so she can write to the guest.

More steps for the unit are in Power and safety.

A guest cannot get in.

  2. Do not give out a door code yourself. Each guest gets a new code, and only the host sends it.
  3. Ask the guest for the booking name and the check-in date. The host checks who they are before helping.
  4. If anyone is in danger, tell them to call 911.

  1. Do not tap the button again.
  2. Ask a mapped person to tap it. Lloyd can.

What does the first line of the card say?

🔴 ALERT. Something needs a person now.

  2. Read each point and the Do: line.
  3. Do what is yours. Pass the rest to Marifel or Lloyd.

Glossary

These are the words we use at Cascade. In other chapters, a marked word shows its meaning when you tap or hover on it.

Balance
The rest of the stay cost after the reservation fee. The guest pays it, with the security deposit, at least one day before check-in.
Brownout
A planned power cut announced by the power company. The team charges the EcoFlow the day before and keeps the emergency light ready.
Cassy
The digital assistant of Cascade Hideaway. She answers guests on Messenger and helps staff in Telegram. From Telegram she never sends anything to a guest.
Concierge
The guest side of Cassy on Messenger. She answers questions and takes bookings, and she passes anything difficult to the host.
Confirm and Leave
The last checks in Phase 4 of the Cleaner app, such as the air conditioner at 24°C, the EcoFlow at 100%, the door lock and the utensil set. Four is the minimum. A turnover or a deep clean shows more, and you confirm every item shown.
Deep clean
A longer, fuller clean of the unit that is done now and then, not after every guest. It has its own choice in the Cleaner app.
Deposit
The PHP 1,000 refundable security deposit on a direct booking. It is paid with the balance and is returned after the check-out inspection finds no problem. Airbnb bookings carry no cash deposit.
Digest
The short morning card the system posts in the OPS group at 08:00. It lists arrivals, departures and anything that needs care that day. On a quiet day it stays silent.
Draft
A reply that Cassy suggests for a guest message. A draft is only text for you to copy. Nothing is sent to the guest until a person sends it.
Dwell time
The waiting time for cleaning products to work on a surface. The Cleaner app has a timer for it in Phase 2, from 5 to 20 minutes.
EcoFlow
A battery power station that stays in the unit. It keeps the important things running during a power cut. It is house equipment, not something a guest can borrow.
Finance group
The Telegram group for Lloyd and Marifel. Money cards, payment receipts and expense entries appear here.
Handoff
A guest request that Cassy cannot settle alone, so she passes it to the host. It appears as a card in the OPS group until someone answers it.
Hold
The dates kept for a guest for 24 hours while the guest sends the reservation fee. If nothing is paid in that time, the hold is released and the guest owes nothing.
Inquiry
A direct booking request from the booking site or Messenger that is not confirmed yet. It becomes a booking when the payment is checked and confirmed.
Ledger
The official record of money in and money out. Confirmed expenses and income are written here.
Mapped staff
A person whose Telegram account is linked to a staff profile. Some buttons, such as Confirm booking, work only for mapped staff. If a button says your account is not mapped, ask Lloyd.
Meter skip
The Cleaner app lets you skip a meter photo now and then, but never two in a row. If the app will not let you skip, take the photo.
Mid-stay clean
A clean during a guest's stay, not after check-out. It has its own choice in the Cleaner app.
Notice
A dated item on the operations board, such as a brownout, a holiday, an event or a reminder. Notices appear in the morning digest.
OPS group
The Telegram group for the whole team, including cleaners. It carries alerts, cleaning reports and guest hand-offs. It never shows money.
Promo
A temporary discount on the nightly price. The owner sets it and it ends on a date.
Rate card
The one stored list of nightly prices. The booking site and Cassy both read it, so a price is changed in one place only.
Receipt
The screenshot or photo that proves a payment. A guest sends it to confirm a booking, and staff post receipt photos of purchases in the Finance group.
Reservation fee
50 percent of the stay, paid to secure the dates once the payment is checked. It is fully refunded when the guest cancels 5 or more days before check-in.
Stay picker
The list in Phase 0 of the Cleaner app where you choose which guest stay this cleaning belongs to.
Supply log
The pop-up in Phase 3 of the Cleaner app where you record the supplies you used. You must save it before you can go on to Phase 4.
System of record
The official record behind the dashboard. It holds bookings, guests, cleaning reports, stock and money. Cards and e-mails are only messages about it.
Turnover
The full clean of the unit after a guest checks out, to get it ready for the next guest.
Work order
A repair or fix task. An urgent note from a cleaner becomes a work order, and it is closed in the dashboard when the job is done.
