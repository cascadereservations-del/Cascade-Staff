Do a turnover

Open the Cleaner app. It takes you from Phase 0 to Phase 4. It will not let you skip a required step.

Open the Cleaner app

Before you start

  • Sign in with your own name and PIN. Never share your PIN.
  • Do not clear your browser data. The app saves your draft on your phone.
  • The app says "Phase 1 of 5" to "Phase 5 of 5". This page says Phase 0 to Phase 4. They are the same screens.
  1. Phase 0 · Setup. 30 seconds Check that Turnover is chosen under Cleaning Type. Check the Cleaning date. Open Which stay is this report for? and choose the stay. Tap Next →.
  2. Phase 1 · Document. 5 photos or more 2 meter photos Do this before you touch anything. Use Capture Photo. Aim for 10 to 12 photos: bedroom, living area, kitchen, appliances, bathroom, entrance. Type both meter readings: electric (kWh) and water (m³). Tap Add Photo under each meter. Tick Meter readings match the photos. Tap All Clear or Found Something. If you found something, write it in Describe what you found. Tap Next →.
  3. Phase 2 · Arrive & Prep. Open the windows. Test the appliances. Look for damage. Check the key card is on its keychain. Strip the bed and towels. Pre-treat stains. Bag the used linen. Pre-spray the toilet, shower, stove and microwave. Do not scrub yet. Tap ▶ Start Timer. 10 min Keep the app open while it runs.
  4. Phase 3 · Clean. 2 kitchen photos or more Clean the bedroom first. Then the kitchen and bathroom. Under Kitchen & Bathroom Photos, show the utensil drawer, the dish rack and the cookware. Tap Open Supply Log. Add what you used, then tap Save Usage. Used nothing? Tap Nothing used this session. You cannot go on without one of these. Spent your own money for the unit? Tap ＋ Add an expense.
  5. Phase 4 · Finish. 5 after photos or more Do the Final Cleaning tasks: vacuum, mop toward the door, wipe the balcony, walk through as the guest, charge the vacuum. Answer every Confirm & Leave item (below). Take 12 to 14 after-cleaning photos. Tap Submit ✓. In Submit report?, tap Submit ✓ again. Wait for Report Submitted.
Other cleaning types

Mid-Stay: a light refresh while the guest stays. Meters are optional. You need 4 photos before and 4 after.

Deep Clean: the same as a Turnover. The last guest name is not required.

Emergency: write an Issue description. Meters, photos and guest name are not required.

The app may offer Submit without meter photos, just this once. Use it only if you cannot go back. The next report needs the photos.

Confirm & Leave

These are the last checks in Phase 4. For each one, tap Confirmed. If something is wrong, tap Issue Found and write what. You cannot submit until every item is answered.

  • Aircon at 24°C. The fridge is cold.
  • Utensil set complete. Count it against the list in the app.
  • EcoFlow at 100%. If it is lower, leave it charging until it is full.
  • Key card and guest code. The card opens the door. The code opens the lock.
  • Smart lock battery. A dim keypad or a beep means low.
  • SmartLife, last. After you lock up, open SmartLife. Tap Smart, then Automations. Switch on Waiting for Check In. If it will not stay on, tap Issue Found.

Before you leave, put both remotes and the key card (on its keychain) on the wooden tray. The tray is on the TV cabinet, below the TV, beside the vase. The app does not ask for this.

Power & Safety card. Guest arrives today before 2 PM: fridge and water heater ON, lights OFF. Guest arrives later, or no guest today: everything OFF, water heater breaker OFF, WiFi and camera circuits ON. Not sure about the fridge? Ask Lloyd or Marifel before you leave. Then tick Final power & safety check.

If the app will not send

The app saves your draft on your phone. With no signal it says No signal — report saved, will retry when connected. If you already tapped Submit ✓, it sends again by itself when the signal returns.

  1. Keep the app open. Wait for Report Submitted.
  2. Tap ↺ Retry if the button shows it. Tap once, then wait.
  3. If you closed the app, open it again. Tap Resume ↺ on Resume session?. Then tap Submit ✓.
  4. Still failing? Write in the OPS group. Do not clear your browser data.

Your draft has no time limit. It stays until you submit or tap Start fresh. A photo marked Failed — tap to retry: tap it.

The standard look

This is how a finished unit looks. Compare each area with its photo.

Bed

Check these 3 things

  1. Pull the sheets tight. No bumps or folds. Tuck in all sides.
  2. Plump every pillow. Put them in a straight, neat row.
  3. No stains on any sheet or pillowcase.

Room

Check these 3 things

  1. Dust from the top down: fan, shelves, bedside tables and skirting boards.
  2. Disinfect the touch points: light switches, door handles, remotes and bedside lamps.
  3. Room items are straight. Mirrors have zero streaks.

Living area

Check these 3 things

  1. Vacuum first, then mop. Work toward the door.
  2. No bad smell. Check the sofa too.
  3. Walk through as the guest. Look for fingerprints, dust and smudges.

TV wall

Check these 3 things

  1. The TV unit is dusted.
  2. The mirror is polished with a dry microfibre cloth. No streaks.
  3. Water a plant only if the soil is completely dry. Push a finger 2 cm into the soil. Not sure? Skip it.

Remote tray

Check these 3 things

  1. Both remotes (aircon and TV) are on the wooden tray, wiped clean.
  2. The key card is on its keychain, on the same tray.
  3. The aircon is set to 24°C before you leave.

Kitchen

Check these 3 things

  1. The dishes are washed. The stove is degreased.
  2. The utensil set is complete. Count it against the list in the Cleaner app.
  3. Flag anything missing, chipped or broken.

Kitchen shelf

Check these 3 things

  1. The microwave (inside and door), the kettle and the rice cooker are clean inside and outside.
  2. The basics are restocked, such as coffee. A guest should never have to ask.
  3. Count the mugs against the list in the app. Flag any that are chipped or missing.

Sink

Check these 3 things

  1. The sink is scrubbed. The faucet shines. The counters are wiped dry.
  2. The dishwashing sponge is a fresh one.
  3. Soap and dish soap are refilled to the standard level.

Bathroom

Check these 3 things

  1. The toilet, shower and sink are scrubbed, rinsed and dry.
  2. Smell-check the toilet. Flush more than once. Wipe the vent.
  3. Shampoo, soap and toilet paper are refilled to the standard level.

Towels

Check these 3 things

  1. Every towel is fresh and has no stains. You pre-treated stains before the wash.
  2. All used towels and sheets are bagged. No used linen is left out.
  3. The towels are folded neatly, as in the photo.

Laundry corner

Check these 3 things

  1. The washing machine is reset. The drum is empty. The door is open.
  2. The cloth steamer is reset. The tank is empty. The cord is coiled. It is back in its spot.
  3. Count the wardrobe hangers. You need at least 8. If there are fewer, flag it.

Entrance

Check these 3 things

  1. Take the Entrance photo from the street. Show the gate and the front of the unit.
  2. Windows are latched and the door is locked.
  3. The key card opens the door. The guest code opens the lock.

What you do in the admin dashboard

The admin dashboard is the record for bookings, payments, stock and cleaning. If a Telegram card and the dashboard disagree, the dashboard is right. Tell Lloyd.

1. Sign in

  1. Type Your name and your 4-digit PIN.
  2. Tap Sign in. You land on Today.
  3. To leave, open the Account menu, tap Sign out.

"No 2FA" and "two-factor" are old text. Ignore them.

2. See what needs attention

  1. Open Today. Read Property readiness, What needs attention, Low stock.
  2. Tap Open on an item to act on it.

A red box means the data did not load. It does not mean all is clear.

3. Confirm a direct booking Messages the guest. No undo.

  1. Check the money in your GCash or bank app. The receipt photo is not proof.
  2. Open Finance, Review queue. Under Payment evidence comparisons type a Reason, tap Approve.
  3. Open Bookings, Inquiries. Tap Confirm, then Confirm in the window.

In Telegram, tap Confirm booking on the receipt card in the Finance group. "Those dates are no longer available or this decision already exists" means nothing changed.

4. Decline a request Final

  1. Open Finance, Review queue. Type a Reason, tap Reject.
  2. Open Bookings, Inquiries. Tap Decline, then Decline in the window.
  3. Write to the guest in Messenger. The dashboard does not.

5. Read a cleaning report

  1. Open Operations, Cleaning log. Tap the date under Cleaned.
  2. Read meters, photos, checklist, and urgent notes in red.
  3. Type a reason. Tap Mark reviewed or Needs follow-up.
  4. Set Outcome: Ready for arrival or Not ready. Tap Record for. This sets the badge on Today.

Override: ready despite blockers needs a written reason. It ignores open work orders.

6. Flag a wrong meter reading Undo for 8 seconds

  1. In the report, pick a Meter flag on that row.
  2. Type a reason. Tap Review. The number is flagged, not edited.

7. Cancel a direct booking Messages the guest. No undo.

  1. Open the booking from Bookings. Tap Cancel booking.
  2. Type a Reason. Tap Cancel booking again. Keep booking backs out.

This frees the dates and tells the guest. It makes no refund. Apply the 5-day rule and ask Lloyd. It works only on direct bookings.

8. Find a booking, guest or report

  1. Tap Search or jump to... or press Ctrl and K.
  2. Type 2 letters or more. Tap a result.
  3. Who is in on a date? Open Bookings, Calendar. Read only.

9. Update stock Undo for 8 seconds

  1. Open Inventory, Stock. Tap the item.
  2. Goods arrived: fill Packs, Cost per pack, Supplier, Date under Receive stock. Tap Record receipt once.
  3. Wrong quantity: fill Change and Reason under Adjust. Tap Record adjustment.

Receiving does not post the expense to Finance. Record that separately. On Counts, Confirm sets the stock and has no undo.

10. Log a repair or a notice Undo for 8 seconds

  1. Repair: open Operations, Work orders. Tap New work order, type a title.
  2. Tick Blocks the next arrival if guests cannot stay. Tap Save.
  3. Warning: open Notices, tap New notice. Fill Title, Type, Audience, Expires. Tap Save.

11. Follow up a guest Undo for 8 seconds

  1. Open Guests, Follow-ups. Tap New follow-up.
  2. Fill Title, Purpose, Priority, Due. Tap Save.

Never screenshot a guest record that shows an ID. The Messenger handoffs tab is history only.

12. Pay the cleaner Posts money. No undo.

  1. Pay Honey first, outside the app.
  2. Send /payclean in the Finance group. Tap the clean.
  3. Check the amount. Tap Mark Paid, or Edit amount and type the number.
  4. Send /notifyclean. Honey taps Acknowledge Payment in the OPS group.

To see what is owed: Operations, Cleaning log, Fees, Unpaid.

Rules of thumb

  • Do Reply to an inquiry within 15 minutes. Use the guest's name.
  • Do Answer a problem within 5 minutes: "Thank you for letting me know. I'm on it." Then write down photos, date, time and what you did.
  • Don't Give a one-word answer. Never ignore a question.
  • Don't Admit liability. Do not promise a refund or a discount.
  • Don't Send check-in details too early. It is a security risk.
  • Don't Pressure a guest for a 5-star review. Keep the ask gentle and optional.

Danger to people

  1. Call 911. Do this first, before anything else.
  2. Fire: call the Bureau of Fire Protection on 160.
  3. Get yourself and any guest to a safe place.
  4. Then tell Lloyd. Write what happened and the time in the OPS group.

Tell Lloyd, then Marifel.

Guest is locked out or the keypad does not respond

  1. Tell Marifel or Lloyd right away. The host must act now.
  2. Do not give a door code. Every guest gets a new code. Only the host sends it.
  3. Ask the guest for the booking name and the check-in date. The host checks who they are.
  4. Tell the guest to message us. We will get them in.

If anyone is in danger, tell them to call 911.

Tell Marifel or Lloyd.

Power is out or a brownout is coming

A brownout is a planned power cut. A notice appears in the OPS group. Near the door, the main breaker is on your left as you face out. The emergency light is on top of the fridge. The EcoFlow is near the TV unit.

The OPS group warned us (planned):

  1. Charge the EcoFlow to full the day before. Keep the emergency light close.
  2. Use the EcoFlow for phones, lights, a fan and the router only.
  3. Never run the aircon or the water heater on it. It will overload.
  4. Write in OPS that you checked the unit and the EcoFlow. Add the time.
  5. When power returns, plug the EcoFlow in again. Check the fridge is cold.

Nobody warned us:

  1. Write in OPS now. Give the time, what you see, and if a guest is inside.
  2. Use the EcoFlow for phones, lights, a fan and the router only.
  3. A guest is inside? Ask Marifel or Lloyd what to tell the guest. Wait for the answer.
  4. Wait until the power is steady before you switch appliances back on.
  5. When power returns, check the fridge is cold. Plug the EcoFlow in and tell OPS it is back.
More detail: EcoFlow battery level
  • 60 to 100%: safe for a long time.
  • 20 to 60%: essentials only. Phone charging and one light.
  • Below 20%: phones only. Keep what is left for an emergency.

Tell The OPS group. Then Lloyd if no notice was posted.

Aircon, Wi-Fi or water problem

Aircon does not cool

  1. Find the remote. It is on the wooden tray on the TV cabinet, below the TV, beside the vase.
  2. Check the setting. After a turnover, the aircon stays at 24 C. A guest is comfortable at 26 C on COOL, fan AUTO.
  3. Check if the whole area has no power or water. Tell the office what you see.

Wi-Fi is down

  1. Unplug the router for 60 seconds. Then plug it in again.
  2. Still down? Tell the office. They check the internet provider for an area outage and update the guest.

Water or plumbing

  1. Never switch the water heater on if the water supply is off. It will burn out.
  2. The toilet flush or a drain misbehaves? Tell the office. Toilet paper goes in the bin beside the toilet, not down the bowl.

Tell Marifel or Lloyd. If a guest is waiting, the office writes to them within 15 minutes.

Damage, or a lost item found

  1. Take a photo before you clean. Show the damage or where you found the item.
  2. Report it in the Cleaner app. Add a note in the issues section.
  3. Do not throw anything away. Keep a lost item safe.
  4. Tell the office. The office decides what happens next.

Tell Marifel or Lloyd.

The Cleaner app will not send

  1. Read the warnings on the Submit screen. They say what is missing: an issue note, a photo that did not upload, or the timer.
  2. Check your signal. Move to a better place or use Wi-Fi.
  3. Do not clear your browser data. Your draft is saved on the phone.
  4. Wait for the signal. The app shows "No signal - report saved, will retry when connected". It sends again by itself. If it does not, tap Submit ✓ once.
  5. Still stuck after two tries? Take a screenshot. Tell Lloyd.

Tell Lloyd. Tell Marifel if a meter photo is the problem.

A guest complains or asks for money back

  1. Listen. Be kind and calm. Do not argue.
  2. Do not promise a refund, a discount or another room. Only the office decides.
  3. Tell Marifel or Lloyd. Write what the guest said and the time.
  4. Tell the guest the office will answer.

Tell Marifel or Lloyd.

A Telegram card I do not understand

  1. Read the first line. Green is normal. Yellow means look today. Red ALERT means act now.
  2. Read the line that starts with Do: That is your one job.
  3. Do not tap a button you do not understand. Send option 1, Send option 2 and Received Payment cannot be undone.
  4. A button says "not mapped"? Nothing happened. Tell Lloyd.

How the gate works

The PIN is checked in this page, on the device. There is no server and no account to lock out. A device that ticks Keep me signed in stays signed in for 30 days; Sign out in the sidebar clears it immediately.

The PIN decides which sections appear — nothing more. This repository is public, so treat the PIN as a way to keep the right screens in front of the right person, not as a lock on the contents. That trade-off is recorded and accepted (D-082).
