Quick guide

Two minutes on telling Cassy what happened, asking her anything, and logging what you spent.

Cassy is our helper inside Telegram. She reads the house calendar, the stock and the house how-tos, and she answers when you ask her by name. She never sends anything to a guest, and she never saves anything until a person taps a button. Talk to each other normally in the group; she only listens when you call her.

Type /keyboard once and a row of buttons stays above the message box. You do not need to remember commands; tap the button and Cassy asks for what she needs.

This is what Cassy tells a guest or prospect on Messenger, from the facts she is given. The wording below is a faithful sketch: she rephrases it every time, in the guest's own language, and signs Cassy, Cascade Concierge. Her voice is calm and precise; she guides and never commands, and she uses no exclamation words.

When the host takes over

Cassy answers from her facts only. If a detail is not in them, she says the host will confirm it personally. She hands over, and the host is alerted, for:

  • Payments and receipts, refunds, cancellations and date changes.
  • Complaints, safety reports, and anything about the door or access.
  • Pets, parties, extra guests and price requests.
  • Anything she cannot resolve or that her facts do not cover.
Once Marifel or Lloyd replies on a chat, Cassy goes quiet. She sends nothing more to that guest for 30 days; the host only gets notices in the app and Telegram. A safety report or a door problem still raises a card for the host, with no line to the guest.

What time is check-in and check-out?

CassyCheck-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 noon. It is a self check-in by smart lock, so a late arrival is easy. Quiet hours run from 10 PM to 6 AM, so we ask for a gentle arrival after 10 PM.

Host takes over: The door PIN is never given in chat. The host sends it at least 24 hours before arrival, once IDs and full payment are in. A guest locked out or asking for the code raises a card to the host at once.

Can we check in early or check out late?

CassyEarly check-in is complimentary from 12 noon when no guest checks out that day. Before noon it is PHP 100 per hour, so 9 AM comes to PHP 300. On a same-day turnover we will do our best to have the unit ready ahead of 2:00 PM and will keep you posted. Late check-out is possible when no one arrives that day; otherwise check-out stays at 12 noon.

Host takes over: Cassy promises nothing until the guest's dates are known and the calendar shows no other guest that day. If there are no dates yet, she asks for them first. The host confirms the actual time.

Is there parking?

CassyYes, parking is free right in front of the unit, inside the gated village, with a security team at the entry points and a camera watching the parking area through the night. It is one vehicle per reservation. The roadside spaces are shared with residents, first come first served. If you arrive after 8 PM, tell us ahead and we will see a space kept for you.

Host takes over: Cassy says plainly that the roadside is shared. A guest who needs a guaranteed slot is asked to let the team know ahead of arrival.

What is the Wi-Fi like, and what is the password?

CassyThe unit has fiber Wi-Fi, steady enough for video calls and streaming, with a dedicated workspace.

Host takes over: Cassy never gives the Wi-Fi password to a prospect. The host shares it after confirmation, and it is released to a verified stay only. Staff can ask Cassy in Telegram: cassy house wifi.

How and when do we pay?

CassyA 50% reservation fee holds the dates once reviewed. The balance and a PHP 1,000 refundable security deposit are due at least one day before check-in, and the deposit is returned after the check-out inspection. Payment is by GCash: the QR already carries the exact amount, and Maya or a bank app can scan it too. When check-in is less than five days away, the full amount and the deposit are requested up front instead of the fee.

Host takes over: Cassy does not confirm a payment. A receipt screenshot or a claim of payment goes to the host, who verifies it and sends the confirmation. A bank transfer (UnionBank InstaPay) is sent by the host by hand.

What if we need to cancel?

CassyFor a direct booking, the reservation fee is fully refunded when you cancel 5 or more days before check-in. Within 5 days it is retained to cover the reserved dates. Airbnb bookings follow Airbnb's own policy.

Host takes over: Cassy states the policy only. A guest asking for money back, or changing or cancelling an actual booking, goes to the host, who reviews it personally.

Are pets allowed?

CassyWe are a pet-free home, which keeps it fresh and allergy-friendly for every guest. If you would like to ask about your pet, we have shared your message with our host, who will reply here personally.

Host takes over: Always. A pet question is a policy exception: Cassy gives the house rule, then the host hears it and decides.

Can we bring extra guests?

CassyThe home is most comfortable for up to 3 adults, or 2 adults with 2 children. Overnight stays are for the guests on the booking, and day visitors are welcome with advance notice. For anything beyond that, we have shared your message with our host.

Host takes over: For 4 adults she suggests a larger place and never says she can accommodate them. Anything past the headcount rules goes to the host. Parties and events get the same treatment: a quiet retreat, then the host.

Where exactly is it, and how do we get there?

CassyCascade Hideaway is inside Bria Homes, Conel Road, Barangay San Isidro, General Santos City, a quiet gated subdivision. Robinsons Place is about 3.7 km (around 10 minutes) and SM City GenSan about 4.1 km. Without a car, Grab Taxi is PHP 120-180 to the malls, Move It or Maxim PHP 10-50, and tricycles wait outside the gate.

Host takes over: The block, lot and map pin are sent by the host after confirmation, never in the first chat. For a fare or landmark she has no figure for, she says the Grab app shows the exact fare, or that the host will confirm.

What about brownouts, and does the aircon keep running?

CassyThe area has occasional scheduled power interruptions. The EcoFlow backup station covers essential devices and keeps the internet equipment running, so you stay connected. It is meant for essentials and connectivity, not for the whole unit, so the aircon does not keep running.

Host takes over: Said plainly before booking. A guest reporting an outage during a stay counts as a complaint: the host is alerted and the service partners are notified. For staff, the brownout notice photo is on the Power interruption card.

Can you do a lower price?

CassyWe completely understand, and we will do our best to accommodate your request. Our direct rates are lower than on Airbnb and the other booking apps, and the nightly rate goes down the longer you stay.

Host takes over: Cassy never counters or names a negotiated number. The host decides personally; Cassy then offers to hold the dates in the meantime.
