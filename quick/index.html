Quick guide

Two minutes on telling Cassy what happened, asking her anything, and logging what you spent.

Cassy is our helper inside Telegram. She reads the house calendar, the stock and the house how-tos, and she answers when you ask her by name. She never sends anything to a guest, and she never saves anything until a person taps a button. Talk to each other normally in the group; she only listens when you call her.

Type /keyboard once and a row of buttons stays above the message box. You do not need to remember commands; tap the button and Cassy asks for what she needs.

What do you need to do?

I finished cleaning

OPS groupEveryone
  1. Finish the checklist in the Cleaner app and send it. The group gets a 🧹 CLEANING card by itself; you do not need to type the report again.
  2. Say so in the group in your own words, so the office knows the unit is ready.
  3. Your fee card comes later. Tap Acknowledge Payment when the money is with you.
Example
You, in OPSDone cleaning, unit is ready 🌿 Report sent.
Cassy🧹 CLEANING · report · Oct 5
Completion 100%. Issues flagged: none.
Cassy, later🧹 Cleaning Fees Settled
Tap to acknowledge you received this payment.
✅ Acknowledge Payment
A card called Payment Ready has a Received Payment button. That one books the fee at once, for whoever taps it. Tap it only when the money is in your hand.

I bought something for the house

OPS: cleanersFinance: office

Cleaners: the OPS group never handles money, so your receipt goes with your cleaning report.

  1. Keep the receipt and take a photo of it.
  2. Add it under extra expenses in your cleaning report. The office gets a card that lists it.
  3. Say it once in the group so nobody forgets. You get it back with your cleaning fee.
Example, cleaner

Or send the receipt photo and tap✅ Confirm
In Finance, a message that starts with a number is saved as an expense. A photo sent there is read as a receipt and shown to you before anything is saved.

Something is broken or missing

OPS groupEveryone
  1. Take a photo of it.
  2. Write it in the issues part of your cleaning report. An urgent one becomes a work order card for the office.
  3. If a guest arrives today, also tell the group now in plain words. Lloyd picks it up from there.
Example
You, in OPSPhoto
Side table is broken and one small bowl is missing. Already in the report.
Cassy, to the office🧹 CLEANING · work order
Issue: side table broken. Next guest: today.
A plain message is for people. Cassy does not read it, and that is fine. Send the photo as a new message, not as a reply to one of her cards.

A guest asks me something

OPS or FinanceEveryone
  1. Ask Cassy for the house how-to. Type cassy house and the thing: wifi, aircon, hot water, parking.
  2. Read her answer and tell the guest in your own words.
  3. If the guest wrote on Messenger or Airbnb and you want the words to send: tap ✍️ Guest reply, then reply to Cassy with the guest's text or a screenshot. Copy her draft and send it yourself.
Example
Youcassy house aircon
CassyThe aircon remote is on the wooden tray on top of the TV cabinet, beside the flower vase. Guests are comfortable at 26 degrees, COOL, fan AUTO.
Do: tell the guest where the remote is.
For a reply to copy, tap✍️ Guest reply
On a 💬 GUEST · handoff card, Send option 1 and Send option 2 message the guest at once, with your name. There is no undo. Read the text first. Everything else Cassy drafts stays a draft until you paste it yourself.

  2. Cassy reads it and lists the times. Tap Save brownout notice. It goes on the board and into the morning card.
  3. Before the day: charge the EcoFlow and keep the emergency light ready. Guests are told only if they ask.

Saving a guest's ID and companions

OPS or FinanceOffice accounts only
  1. Send the ID photo, or a screenshot of the guest chat, with the caption /guest.
  2. Tap the guest it belongs to. If the name is not on the list, tap Other guest and type part of the name.
  3. Read the card. It says exactly what will be kept: from an ID, the name and the ID type only, never the number; from a chat, the names and the mobile number. Tap Save. Nothing is saved before that.
Example
YouPhoto of the ID
/guest
CassyNothing is saved yet. Whose photo is this? Tap the guest it belongs to.
Guest arriving Oct 9🔎 Other guest❌ Cancel
This works only for a Telegram account that Lloyd has linked to an owner or admin login. Anyone else sees "not linked" and nothing is read or saved. Never type an ID number into the chat.

Asking for my pay

Staff appCleaners
  1. Open Payment Request in the Staff app. Your cleans that are not paid yet are listed.
  2. Tick the cleans. Switch on +₱150 transport only for the ones where you paid for it. Add anything you bought, with a photo of the receipt if you have one.
  3. Read the summary, then tap Send to Finance. Finance pays you and the office posts PAID in the OPS group.
  4. Tap Received when the money is in your account.

Which group?

OPS group

Everyone
  • The house, the guests, the cleaning, power and stock.
  • The morning card, guest hand-offs, cleaning cards, rain and brownout notices.
  • Money is never shown here. Only your own fee card shows a fee, because you need it.

Finance group

Owner and admin
  • Expenses, receipt photos, cleaner fees to pay, refunds and reports.
  • Booking receipts to confirm, and the weekly system check.
  • Cassy shows money here and can prepare an expense card for one tap.
Unsure? Use OPS. A Finance command typed in OPS gets no reply at all; that is normal, not a fault. Type /menu in either group to see its buttons.

If Cassy doesn't understand

Try this, in order

  1. Ask again in one full sentence that starts with cassy. She remembers only the last few lines, so a short follow-up can lose her.
  2. For a house how-to, use cassy house and one word, like cassy house wifi.
  3. Tap /menu and use a button instead of typing.
  4. If she says "That card is not waiting for an answer", you replied to an old card. Send a new message instead.
  5. If she says "I could not reach the model", wait a minute and try again. Still wrong? Tell Lloyd in the group.
Example
Youand the water?
CassyI am not sure what you mean about the water.
Do: ask me in one full sentence, like cassy house hot water.
